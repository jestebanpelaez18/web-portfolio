Summary

Arabian Fragrance is a small perfume company in Helsinki that sells online and in its own shop. They needed a store that felt like the brand, helped customers understand scent profiles and concentrations, and stayed easy for the team to update.

I built the storefront from scratch in Next.js, independent of Shopify themes, and connected it to Shopify for products, prices, availability and editorial content. The team edits the catalog in Shopify. The design and behaviour live in code.

Highlights

Search built for fragrance

Ranked matches across names, notes and collections, with English, Finnish and Swedish aliases. Every meaningful word in the query has to match.

Three languages

English, Finnish and Swedish routes, interface text and product metadata, with filters that behave the same in every translation.

Content the team can edit

Homepage sections, Our Story and the showroom page come from Shopify metaobjects, sanitized before they render.

One inventory

The online store and the physical shop share stock and sales through Shopify, after moving away from a separate Stripe setup.

Recommendations that make sense for perfume

Related products are ranked by shared scent accords, collection and gender, with stable tie-breaking so the same page always shows the same suggestions.

Layering suggestions work differently: the store team picks complementary products in Shopify, and the page shows their choice.

Checked before every release

GitHub Actions runs lint, formatting and type checks, unit tests, a production build and a browser suite on every change. The browser tests cover catalog interactions, search, product pages, the shopping bag, translations and responsive navigation.
